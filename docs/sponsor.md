# Become a Sponsor

PowerGIS is free and open-source software, released under the
[Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/opengeos/PowerGIS/blob/main/LICENSE) and developed in
the open. There is no paid tier or seat licence. The desktop app and command line collect no
analytics or usage telemetry; the hosted websites use analytics, as described in [Privacy](privacy.md).

That model only works if the work behind it is sustainable. If PowerGIS saves you or your team
time, sponsorship is the most direct way to keep it moving.

[Sponsor on GitHub](https://github.com/sponsors/giswqs){ .md-button .md-button--primary }
[Buy me a coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/giswqs){ .md-button }

## Ways to sponsor

| Option | What it is | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/giswqs)** | Monthly or one-time sponsorship, billed through your existing GitHub account. | Individuals and organizations that want recurring support, and companies that can expense it alongside other GitHub spend. |
| **[Buy Me a Coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/giswqs)** | A quick one-off contribution, no account required. | A small thank-you for a feature or bug fix that helped you. |

Both go to [Qiusheng Wu](https://github.com/giswqs), the maintainer of PowerGIS and the wider
[opengeos](https://github.com/opengeos) ecosystem, including [GeoLibre](https://geolibre.app).

## What your sponsorship supports

- **Development time**: new features from the
  [roadmap](https://github.com/opengeos/PowerGIS/blob/main/ROADMAP.md), bug fixes, and keeping
  up with fast-moving dependencies such as egui, wgpu and the GeoLibre tool suite.
- **Shipping to every platform**: the Apple developer account, code signing and notarization
  behind the macOS app, the Microsoft Store listing, and building the Windows and Linux
  [packages](installation.md) and the [Python package](python.md), which the project pays for
  whether or not anyone sponsors it.
- **Hosting**: [powergis.app](https://powergis.app), the [web app](https://web.powergis.app), the
  [plugin registry](https://plugins.powergis.app), the
  [download mirror](https://downloads.powergis.app) and the sample data and fonts at
  assets.powergis.app.
- **Documentation**: the [User Guide](user-guide/index.md) and examples that make the app usable
  without training.
- **Support**: triaging issues, answering questions and reviewing community pull requests.

## Sponsoring as an organization

If your team depends on PowerGIS and you would like to discuss a larger or invoiced sponsorship,
please [open an issue](https://github.com/opengeos/PowerGIS/issues/new/choose) to get in touch.
Grant-funded and institutional support is welcome too.

## Other ways to support PowerGIS

Sponsorship is not the only currency, and none of these cost anything:

- **Star and share**: [star the repository](https://github.com/opengeos/PowerGIS) and tell
  colleagues who are looking for a fast, open-source GIS.
- **Report bugs well**: a reproducible [issue](https://github.com/opengeos/PowerGIS/issues) is
  worth a great deal.
- **Contribute code or plugins**: see [Contributing](contributing.md) and
  [Plugins](plugins.md#writing-a-plugin).
- **Translate the interface**: PowerGIS ships in 19 languages; see [Languages](i18n.md) to add or
  improve one.
